# ECC Commercial Licence Agreement (Draft Outline)

**DRAFT FOR DISCUSSION ONLY. NOT A SIGNABLE AGREEMENT.**

This outline is a drafting checklist. It does not grant rights, create payment obligations or replace a lawyer-reviewed agreement.

## 2. Licensed product

- Product: Enterprise Control Center (ECC)
- Edition: [COMMERCIAL / ENTERPRISE]
- Version or release range: [VERSION]
- Licensed components: [LIST]
- Excluded components: [LIST]

Third-party components are licensed under their own notices and terms.

## 3. Grant of rights

Subject to payment and compliance with this agreement, the licensor grants the customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right for the term to install and use the specified ECC components for the purposes and within the deployment scope stated in the order form.

No ownership of the software or intellectual property is transferred.

Any right to modify, create derivative works, deploy for customers, redistribute, sublicense, white-label, host for third parties or resell must be expressly selected in the order form. Silence does not grant those rights.

## 4. Licence scope

The order form must specify:

- Licensed legal entities and affiliates.
- Number of production instances and permitted environments.
- Whether subsidiaries, contractors and service providers may access the system.
- Permitted users, sites and territories, if limits apply.
- Internal-use or external-client deployment rights.
- Backup, disaster recovery, testing and development rights.

Do not impose technical limits that are absent from the signed order form.

## 5. Fees and term

- Licence fee: [AMOUNT AND CURRENCY]
- Billing: [ANNUAL / OTHER]
- Term: [START DATE] to [END DATE]
- Renewal: [RENEWAL RULE]
- Payment due: [NUMBER] days after invoice
- Taxes: [TAX TREATMENT]

A renewal or price change must follow the notice and acceptance procedure in the final agreement.

## 6. Optional reseller or revenue-share terms

These terms apply only if expressly included in a signed order form:

- Revenue-share rate: [PERCENTAGE]
- Revenue base: [DEFINED GROSS OR NET RECEIPTS]
- Permitted deductions: [EXPLICIT LIST]
- Reporting period: [QUARTERLY / OTHER]
- Statement due date: [NUMBER] days after period end
- Payment due date: [NUMBER] days after statement
- Records retention: [PERIOD]
- Audit notice and scope: [DEFINED LIMITS]
- Audit cost allocation: [DEFINED THRESHOLD AND RULE]

No percentage, reporting duty or audit right is created by this draft alone.

## 7. Restrictions

Except where the signed order form or applicable law permits, the customer must not:

- Resell, sublicense, publish or redistribute licensed proprietary components.
- Remove copyright, attribution or licence notices.
- Misrepresent ownership of ECC or its components.
- Circumvent technical licence checks for the purpose of obtaining unlicensed commercial features.
- Use the licensed components outside the agreed scope.

Restrictions must not purport to remove rights that cannot legally be restricted or override third-party open-source licences.

## 8. Customer materials and data

The customer retains rights in its data and materials. The agreement must define whether the licensor may process operational data for support, diagnostics or service delivery, and must address security, confidentiality, retention, deletion and applicable data-protection requirements.

## 9. Support and updates

- Support level: [LEVEL]
- Support hours and response targets: [DETAILS]
- Updates included: [DETAILS]
- End-of-life policy: [DETAILS]

## 10. Warranties and liability

The final agreement must state any express warranties, permitted disclaimers, liability cap, excluded losses and exceptions required by applicable law. No clause should attempt to exclude liability where such exclusion is prohibited.

## 11. Termination

Define termination for material breach, non-payment, insolvency where permitted, and expiry. State the cure period, effect of termination, permitted transition period, data export and deletion process, and treatment of perpetual rights if any are granted.

## 12. General terms

The final agreement must address governing law, jurisdiction, dispute resolution, assignment, subcontracting, notices, force majeure, severability, entire agreement and amendment procedure.
